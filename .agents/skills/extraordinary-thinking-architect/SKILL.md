# Extraordinary Thinking & First-Principles Architecture

## Thinking Framework
1. **Deconstruct to First Principles**: Strip away assumptions and legacy constraints to identify the fundamental truths of the problem.
2. **Out-of-the-Box Ideation**: Brainstorm multiple non-obvious solutions before settling on implementation.
3. **Elevated Craftsmanship**: Deliver solutions that go beyond basic functional requirements, surprising and delighting users with exceptional Polish, performance, and elegance.
